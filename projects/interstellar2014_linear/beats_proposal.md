TWIST DISCIPLINE (Joker / Parasite pattern preserved)
================================================================================
Interstellar's engine is the LATE TIME-LOOP REVEAL: the "ghost" that knocks books
off Murph's shelf and spells STAY / the coordinates in the dust is COOPER HIMSELF,
reaching back through the tesseract from inside the black hole. The film withholds
this until ~02:26.
- HOOK (first ~30-45s) and the whole Act 1 setup must present the ghost as an
  UNEXPLAINED MYSTERY - a strange presence in Murph's room, gravity behaving oddly.
  Do NOT say or imply it is Cooper. The mystery is the bait; the loop is the payoff.
- The tesseract reveal beat (B29-B30, ~02:22-02:28) is where narration turns: the
  ghost was Cooper all along; love and gravity carried the message across time.
- The Miller's-wave (B17), Romilly's-23-years (B19), Mann's-betrayal (B23-B25), and
  the Gargantua sacrifice (B27-B28) carry the peril/violence -> censor is LIGHT here
  (this is a PG-13 film; mostly whole-frame only if a face-crack or blood shows).
- Ending ambiguity (B34): Cooper leaves Cooper Station to find Amelia Brand, who is
  alone on Edmunds' planet starting the colony. State it as his CHOICE to go, and a
  hopeful maybe-reunion - do NOT state they reunite (the film cuts before that).
  (Mirror Joker U19/U20 + Parasite ending ambiguity discipline.)
