STYLE REMINDER (linear mode)
================================================================================
- Follow the FILM'S OWN ORDER. One cold-open hook exception allowed (pull 1-2
  shots forward to grab attention), then stay strictly chronological.
- Coverage over selection: walk the connected chain (grief -> the puppy -> the
  theft/assault -> the hunt -> escalation -> the Tarasov war -> resolution).
- Name + role every key player on first appearance (Viggo the crime boss; Iosef
  his reckless son; Marcus the old friend sent to kill John; Winston the
  Continental's proprietor).
- Action set-pieces earn the most shots. Do NOT narrate a whole gunfight in one
  line over one shot - that is the "narrated not shown" failure. Break each fight
  into its stages ([S6], 3-4+ shots minimum).
- No invented backstory. The film tells you exactly who John WAS through others'
  fear of him ("Baba Yaga"); present what the film shows, don't over-explain.

             chars: John

             chars: John, Marcus (bg)

COVERAGE / DEPTH CHECKLIST (Stage 2 must satisfy)
================================================================================
- 30 beats over 4 acts. For fuller_recap (90-110 shots) that is ~3-4 shots/beat.
  The dialogue/setup beats can run 2-3; the ACTION beats (B11, B13, B19, B21, B22,
  B23, B26, B28) MUST run 3-4+ ([S6]) to hit depth WITHOUT padding narration.
- If Stage 2 lands under ~90 shots, the shortfall is almost certainly under-
  catalogued action set-pieces - go back and enumerate each fight's stages.
- Reaction/consequence shots matter: Iosef's fear, Viggo's dread, Marcus's
  divided loyalty - catalogue them so motive is shown, not just stated.
- HOOK (B01): high engagement, open the "who is this man / why is he dying" gap;
  do NOT front-load the "Baba Yaga" reveal - let B10 land it.
- Ending ambiguity is light here (he lives, gets a dog) - state it as a choice to
  keep living, don't over-sentimentalize.
