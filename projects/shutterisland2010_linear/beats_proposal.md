    - that usage is part of the mystery and is FINE. What is forbidden is any
    narration EQUATING Laeddis with Teddy/the protagonist before B24.

R2. RACHEL SOLANDO = DOLORES CHANAL (anagram); the "missing patient" case is a

    - Lands on the same whiteboard: B24 (~6600s / 01:50:00) - "RACHEL SOLANDO =
      DOLORES CHANAL".
    HOLD: the missing-patient investigation must be narrated straight through
    B01-B23. Do not hint the names are linked or that Rachel "isn't real."

R3. CHUCK IS DR. LESTER SHEEHAN - Teddy's treating psychiatrist, not his partner.
    - Lands at the lighthouse: B25 (~6758s / 01:52:37) - "I'm Lester Sheehan."
    HOLD: Chuck is Teddy's Marshal partner through B01-B24. Noyce's warning "don't

R4. THE WHOLE "INVESTIGATION" IS A STAGED ROLEPLAY by the hospital staff to break
    Teddy's delusion (Cawley's radical last-chance therapy).
    - Explained at the lighthouse: B24-B26 (~01:50-01:58).
    HOLD: everything before reads as a real investigation with an uncooperative
    staff. Do not frame the staff's evasions as "part of a plan" earlier.

R5. THE TRUE CRIME: Dolores (manic-depressive) drowned their three children;
    Andrew/Teddy then shot her. The "arsonist killed my wife in a fire" story is his
    own protective delusion.
    - The lake-house flashback delivers it: B26 (~6955s / 01:55:54).
    HOLD: through B01-B25, the wife "died in an apartment fire set by the arsonist
    Andrew Laeddis." Do NOT reveal the children, the drowning, or that Teddy killed
    her before B26.

R6. THE ENDING LINE - "which would be worse: to live as a monster, or to die as a
    good man?"
    - B28 (~7761s / 02:09:20). *** REPRODUCE THE AMBIGUITY, ADD NO INTERPRETATION. ***

      relapsed or deliberately chose the lobotomy, must NOT gloss it as "choosing
      death/oblivion" or "escaping his guilt," must NOT interpret his state of mind.
      Leave the viewer exactly where the film leaves them. This ambiguity is the
      single most important thing in the film - any resolution of it is a failure.
