## Bai can lam

Nhin anh va dien 3 cot cho TUNG shot duoi day. Khong co dap an o day.

### `depicts` (chi dung tu trong day, 1-2 tu moi shot)

`action`, `aftermath`, `dialogue`, `discovery`, `emotion`, `establishing`, `montage`, `reaction`, `reveal`, `setup`, `transition`

Xem `docs/TAGGING_GUIDE.md`, va **bat chuoc quy uoc trong cac vi du o tren** - nhat la khi mot cua so co nhieu khoanh khac thi chon cai nao lam chinh.

### `chars`

Ten nhan vat NHIN THAY RO trong BAT KY frame nao. Khong chac -> `(khong ro)`.

### `action`

Mot cau ta cai dang xay ra tren man hinh.
