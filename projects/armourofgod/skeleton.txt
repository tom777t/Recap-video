STORY SKELETON — Chinese Zodiac (CZ12 / Armour of God 3, 2012)
Stage 6A audit. Beats only; validate STRUCTURE before writing prose.

THE QUESTION CHAIN (each beat answers the prior question, opens the next):

HOOK: A man freefalls toward an erupting volcano with no parachute — to save
      a chunk of bronze.                                  -> Why would anyone do that?
1. INCITING: China's 12 looted zodiac heads are worth millions. MP Corporation
   hires JC, the world's best treasure hunter, to grab the missing ones.
                                                          -> Can he actually get them?
2. PROOF / HEIST 1 (castle): JC infiltrates a French chateau, steals the rooster
   head, escapes the wingsuit drop + hedge-maze chase.    -> Who's he stealing from?
3. THE OWNER: The castle belongs to Katherine, a bankrupt aristocrat whose
   ancestor looted that very palace in 1860 — and then vanished at sea.
                                                          -> Where did he vanish?
4. THE CONSCIENCE: Coco, a Chinese activist on JC's trail, confronts him: these
   aren't loot to sell, they're stolen history.           -> Will JC keep selling them?
5. THE TRAIL: Katherine's ancestor's lost ship "Indestructible" points to a
   remote island. JC's team sails there hunting his treasure.  -> What's on the island?
6. ISLAND / PIRATES: Pirates (the Vulture) ambush them; a jungle brawl; they find
   the wreck, the ancestor's bones — and 8 tons of gold.  -> Does greed win, or conscience?
7. THE TURN: Surrounded by stolen-treasure guilt (Katherine's + Coco's), JC
   decides to give the heads back to China, not MP.       -> How will MP retaliate?
8. ESCALATION: MP, furious, stages a stunt: dump the last dragon head into an
   active volcano live on air unless the world pays.      -> Can JC save it in time?
9. CLIMAX (skydive/volcano): JC skydives WITHOUT a parachute, fights for the
   dragon head over the lava, and recovers it.            -> What happens to MP?
10. RESOLUTION: MP's Morgan + son are arrested (the stolen Roses painting found);
    JC anonymously mails every looted relic back to its home nation; treasures
    repatriated worldwide.                                -> (closes the loop the hook opened)

STRUCTURAL AUDIT (the 5 questions):
1. Does every beat cause the next? YES — heist -> owner -> her guilt + Coco's
   pressure -> the trail to the island -> gold tempts -> conscience turns him ->
   MP retaliates -> climax -> justice. Each is a consequence, not a list.
2. Any beat present ONLY because it's in the movie? The David/Bonnie marriage
   subplot and Professor Guan kidnapping are minor — INCLUDE only as quick texture,
   not as beats. The 1860 sacking flashback EARNS its place (it's the WHY behind
   the whole moral spine), so keep it, but brief.
3. Missing transition? Risk at 6->7 (gold found -> gives it back): must SHOW the
   turn is caused (Katherine's vanished-ancestor guilt + Coco's challenge), not
   just announced. And 7->8: MP must learn JC is betraying them before the volcano
   stunt. Add setup lines for both.
4. Would a first-timer get A->B each time? Yes IF: name JC's job up front (beat 1),
   name Coco's motive (beat 4) before she changes him (beat 7), and establish the
   island comes FROM the ancestor's ship (beat 5) before the pirates (beat 6).
5. Each beat answer a Q and open a new one? Yes — see the chain above; the hook's
   "why jump?" is only paid off at beat 9, pulling the viewer the whole way.

ACTION SET-PIECES (must be Setup -> Action -> Consequence, families capped <=2):
- castle_wingsuit + hedge_maze (beat 2): setup = the rooster-head heist target.
- jungle_fight (beat 6): setup = pirates ambush the team for the gold.
- skydive + volcano_lava (beat 9): setup = MP's "dump it in the volcano" threat.
Each family used <=2; no knife/repeat-style families here. Verdict: SKELETON FLOWS.
