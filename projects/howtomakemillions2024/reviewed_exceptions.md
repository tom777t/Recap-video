# Reviewed exceptions — Gia Tài Của Ngoại / How to Make Millions Before Grandma Dies (2024)

Findings **looked at and deliberately kept**. Each still prints on every run and
stays visible in the report; it just is not counted as a problem.

Not a mute button: the key names the exact finding (here the beat id), never the
gate; a blanket entry is refused by the parser; an entry with no date or reason
is refused; and if the finding stops occurring the run reports it **STALE** and
asks for deletion, so a leftover note cannot cover the next one.

```reviewed-exceptions
DEPTH-UNDER | B26 | 2026-09-05 | The train home (01:02:15-01:03:11) is deliberately ONE shot. Amah at the window, M watching her, nothing said. Per I2 this is the register the film works in - relationship, not event - and the disambiguation test in RULES A9/I2 says cutting it would cost the sense of having sat in the moment, not an event. Splitting it into 2-3 shots to satisfy the per-beat floor would be inventing coverage to quiet a gate (B8). Argued in beats_proposal.md before the gate was consulted, not after.
```

## Provenance, and one correction

This was the **only** reviewed finding recorded in prose in this project. It is
stated twice in `beats_proposal.md` — once at the beat entry ("B26 is
deliberately a single shot (DEPTH-UNDER flagged and reviewed)") and once in the
Stage-2 summary ("one beat under the floor of 2 — B26 at 1 shot, which is the
deliberate single-shot train-home beat already argued for above").

**There is no kept DEPTH-REACTION finding on this project.** `beats_proposal.md`
records the opposite: DEPTH-CLIMAX, DEPTH-REACTION and DEPTH-MOTIF *"have
nothing to iterate and cannot"* run here at all. The kept DEPTH-REACTION belongs
to **badgenius2017, beat B59** — see that project's own file. And B54 in Bad
Genius was the case that was **fixed** rather than kept: the gate pointed at a
real gap and a 0.75s burst turned out to contain the missing shot.
