# Reviewed exceptions — Bad Genius (2017)

Findings **looked at and deliberately kept**. Each still prints on every run and
stays visible in the report; it just is not counted as a problem.

Not a mute button: the key names the exact finding (here the beat id), never the
gate; a blanket entry is refused by the parser; an entry with no date or reason
is refused; and if the finding stops occurring the run reports it **STALE** and
asks for deletion, so a leftover note cannot cover the next one.

DEPTH-REACTION | B59 | 2026-09-05 | The pencils being printed while the exam runs (01:28:45). The gate is right that the beat has action coverage, no reaction shot, and two declared characters. The only candidate reaction frame in the window (01:28:38, Grace and Pat's faces close together) sits between two press inserts and is cut against Sydney, so it is not a clean single-moment shot. ## Why only this one

Two DEPTH-REACTION findings were reviewed on this project and they went opposite
ways, which is the point of recording both:

- **B54** — the gate pointed at a **real gap**. A 0.75s burst turned out to
  contain the missing shot (01:21:39, Lynn's reflection in the open panel). It
  was added. **Not an exception — a fix.** This is the gate working.
- **B59** — kept, above.

Only findings that are *kept* belong in this file. A finding that was fixed
leaves no trace here, and should not.
