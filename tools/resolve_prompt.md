# ACT ON THE REVIEW of one act of a film-recap narration

Independent readers filed findings on these lines. For EVERY finding (by its
key) reply RESOLVED with what you changed, or ANSWERED with why the reading
is wrong (at least 8 words - a dismissal closes nothing). Change ONLY
the lines the findings name; return each changed line whole, by id. Keep the
line's job, its beat and its length (its picture is fixed). A RESOLVED with
no changed line is not resolved. Reply with ONLY the JSON object.

Each line shows its SLOT in brackets - its role in the layout (ACTION,
QUOTE, BRIDGE, REACTION VOICE / EVAL, OUTRO, INTRO) and whether it sits on an
ANCHOR. A rewrite keeps that role: a REACTION stays heard as the narrator, an
ACTION or BRIDGE carries no I / you / question / stance adverb, nothing on an
anchor but its REACTION passes judgement, the OUTRO stays thesis then call to
action. Labels (payload, delivery) are set by code from the slot - do not
change them. A rewrite that breaks its slot is NOT saved and comes back to you.

A finding whose kind is `gate` comes from the draft's own checks, not from a
reader: fix the lines it names. It needs no response entry.
